# Monytar — Complete User Guide

Monytar is where your company tracks who spent what, gets it approved, and keeps records of what's been paid back. This guide explains everything you can do in Monytar, in plain language, for people who have never used an expense system before.

**How to use this guide:** you don't need to read it start to finish. Use the [table of contents](#table-of-contents) to jump to what you need, or start with [Quick Start](#quick-start) if you're brand new.

> Every button, tab, and field name in this guide is written exactly as it appears in Monytar, so you can match it to what's on your screen.

## Quick Start

If you're brand new to Monytar, do these five things and you'll understand the whole system:

1. **Sign in.** Go to the Monytar login page and enter the email and password your admin set up for you (see [Signing In](#signing-in)).
2. **Check who you're signed in as.** Look at the bottom of the left menu (or your account menu on mobile) — it shows your name, your role, and your department. This tells you what you're allowed to do in Monytar.
3. **Find your way around.** The left menu (sidebar) lists every page available to your role. Click **Dashboard** to see your overview at any time.
4. **Add your first expense.** Click **Requests** in the left menu, then **New request**. Fill in the amount and why you spent it, then click **Submit for Approval**.
5. **Watch it move through approval.** Go back to **Requests** to see its status change from Pending to Approved (or Rejected). You'll also get a notification.

That's the whole loop: submit → get approved → get paid. Everything else in Monytar supports that loop.

## Roles in Monytar

Monytar has exactly four roles. Your admin assigns your role — you can't change it yourself.

| Role | What they mainly do |
|---|---|
| **Employee** | Submits their own expenses and tracks them. |
| **Manager** | Everything an Employee can do, plus approves expenses from people in their own department. |
| **Finance** | Everything a Manager can do, plus approves any expense in the company, marks expenses as paid, manages vendor approval, expense categories, and views Reports. |
| **Admin** | Full access — everything above, plus managing users, departments, organization settings, and billing. |

Every role can submit their own expenses, view their own Requests, see Notifications, and use Settings. What differs is what else shows up in the left menu.

## Signing In

Go to the login page and enter your **Email** and **Password**, then click **Sign in**.

- **Forgot your password?** Click **Forgot?** next to the Password field. Enter your email on the **Forgot password?** page and Monytar will email you a reset link. Click the link, then set a new password (at least 8 characters) on the **Set a new password** page.
- **"Incorrect email or password"** — double-check what you typed, or use **Forgot?** to reset it.
- **"Your email address isn't verified yet"** — open the confirmation email Monytar sent you and click the link inside, then come back and sign in.
- If you're returned to the login page with a message like *"You've been signed out"*, *"Your session expired"*, or *"We couldn't restore your session"* — this isn't an error you caused. Just sign in again.
- If you see *"This account has been deactivated. Contact your administrator"* — an admin has turned off your access. You'll need to ask them to reactivate it.

## Getting Your Account Set Up

There are two ways people get access to Monytar:

### You were invited by your company's admin

You'll get an email invitation. Click the link in it — it opens a **Join [Your Company]** page that already shows your email and the role you were invited as. Enter your **Full name** and choose a **Password** (at least 8 characters), then click **Accept invitation**. Depending on how your company's Monytar is set up, you'll either land straight on your dashboard, or you'll be asked to check your email for one more confirmation link before you can sign in.

### You're setting up a brand-new company account

Go to **Sign up**. It's a short wizard with four steps:

1. **Account Details** — your name, work email, and password.
2. **Organization** — your company's details.
3. **Choose Plan** — pick a plan (see [Admin Guide → Billing](#billing-admin--finance)).
4. **Get Started** — confirms you're set up.

Signing up this way makes you the first user of a brand-new organization, so you'll automatically be its Admin.

## Your Dashboard

Click **Dashboard** in the left menu at any time to return to your overview. What you see depends on your role.

**Everyone sees:**
- A **Spending Trend** chart — monthly totals over the last 6 months.
- A **By Category** chart — how spending breaks down by expense category.
- **Recent Requests** — your latest submissions (or, for Manager/Finance/Admin, the whole company's latest).

**If you're an Employee**, your stat cards are: **Pending** (how many requests you have awaiting a decision), **Approved** (total value approved so far), **Rejected** (how many were turned down), and **This Month** (total you've submitted this month). You'll also see a **New Request** button at the top and a **Top Vendors** chart of who you spend with most.

**If you're a Manager, Finance, or Admin**, your stat cards are: **Pending** (company-wide requests awaiting approval, with the total value), **Approved**, **Paid**, and a fourth card that depends on your role — **Users** (Admin), **Awaiting Pay** (Finance), or **Team** (Manager, showing your department's headcount). You'll also see **Department Budgets** (how much of each budget has been used) and, if there are any expenses waiting on you, a **Pending Approvals** card.

**If you're an Admin** on a new organization, you'll also see a **Getting started** card at the top with four steps: *Create a department*, *Invite your team*, *Add a vendor*, and *Submit your first expense*. Each one is a shortcut to the right page, and it checks itself off automatically as you do them. Click the **✕** to dismiss it — it also disappears on its own once all four are done.

## Managing Expenses (Requests)

In Monytar, every expense you submit is called a **Request** (short for "expense request"). Click **Requests** in the left menu to see them.

- If you're an **Employee**, this page is titled **My Requests** and only shows your own.
- If you're a **Manager, Finance, or Admin**, it's titled **All Requests** and shows everyone's — but you can still submit and track your own expenses from here too.

### How to add an expense

**When to use this:** whenever you've paid for something for work — or need to ask for pre-approval to spend on something — and need Monytar to have a record of it.

**Steps:**
1. Click **Requests** in the left menu.
2. Click **New request** (top right).
3. Under **Amount & Category**, enter the **Amount** (in your organization's currency, shown next to the label) and pick a **Category**.
4. Under **Purpose & Details**, write your **Business Justification** — why this expense was needed. This is required.
5. Optionally pick a **Vendor** — who you paid. Only approved, active vendors show up here (see [Vendors](#vendors)).
6. Optionally set a **Priority** (Low, Medium, High, or Urgent) — this is shown to whoever reviews it, as context.
7. Check or adjust the **Expense Date** (defaults to today) and, optionally, a **Due Date**.
8. Add any **Additional Notes** if useful.
9. Attach your receipt(s) — see [Adding a receipt](#how-to-add-a-receipt) below.
10. Click **Save as Draft** to keep working on it later, or **Submit for Approval** to send it in now.

**What happens next:** if you click **Submit for Approval**, Monytar checks the amount against your organization's rules. Small expenses may be **auto-approved** instantly with no reviewer at all. Otherwise it's sent into the approval chain (see [Understanding expense status](#understanding-expense-status)) and you'll get a notification once someone decides.

**Tip:** you don't have to have everything ready at once. Click **Save as Draft** and come back to finish it later — a draft never gets sent to anyone until you submit it.

### How to add a receipt

**When to use this:** any time you have a photo or PDF of your receipt, invoice, or proof of purchase.

**Steps:**
1. On the **New Expense Request** form (or an existing request's page), find the **Receipts** section.
2. Click **Click to upload receipts**.
3. Choose one or more files.

**Accepted files:** JPG, PNG, WEBP, HEIC, or PDF, up to 10 MB each. Anything else (like a Word document, or a file over 10 MB) will be rejected with an explanation.

**What happens next:** the file appears in a list with its name and size, with a small **✕** to remove it before you save if you picked the wrong one. Once saved, it stays attached to the request permanently as your proof of purchase.

**Tip:** if your organization requires a receipt above a certain amount, Monytar will show a warning right on the form and block **Submit for Approval** until you attach one (or you can still **Save as Draft** and add it later).

### How to edit an expense

You can only edit a request that's still **your own**, and only while it's a **Draft** or has been **Rejected**.

**Steps:**
1. Open the request from **Requests**, or from its **Edit** button.
2. This opens the same form you used to create it, pre-filled with what you entered.
3. Make your changes and click **Save as Draft** or **Submit for Approval** again.

Once a request is **Pending**, **Approved**, or **Paid**, it can no longer be edited — that's Monytar protecting the record once someone else has acted on it (or is about to).

### How to submit an expense

If you have a request that's still a **Draft**, open it. You'll see a **Draft** card explaining it hasn't been submitted yet, with an **Edit** button and a **Submit for Approval** button. Click **Submit for Approval** when you're ready to send it in.

### Understanding expense status

Every request has a status, shown as a colored badge:

| Status | What it means | What happens next |
|---|---|---|
| **Draft** | You've started it but haven't submitted it. Only you can see and edit it. | Nothing happens until you submit it. |
| **Pending** | It's been submitted and is waiting for a decision. | A manager and/or finance reviewer needs to approve or reject it (or it may auto-approve instantly if it's small enough). |
| **Approved** | A reviewer signed off on it. | If you used a company card, it just becomes part of your record. If you paid personally, Finance or an Admin still needs to mark it **Paid** (see [Getting Paid Back](#getting-paid-back-payments)). |
| **Rejected** | A reviewer turned it down, with a reason. | You can revise it and resubmit — see below. |
| **Paid** | Finance or an Admin has recorded that payment was made. | Nothing further — this is the end of the line. |

> An expense can also auto-approve instantly, with no reviewer at all, if your organization has an **auto-approve threshold** set and your amount is under it. Check the request's **Approval Chain** panel — it explicitly says *"Auto-approved — Amount is below the organization's auto-approval threshold"* when this happens.

### Handling rejected expenses

If your request was rejected, open it. You'll see the reviewer's comment under **Reviewer Comment**, and a **Revise & Resubmit** card. Read the reason, click **Edit** to fix whatever was wrong, then click **Resubmit** to send it back into the approval process from the start.

### Tracking expenses

On the **Requests** page:

- Use the tabs — **All**, **Drafts**, **Pending**, **Approved**, **Rejected**, **Paid** — to filter by status. Each tab shows a count.
- Use the **Search by number, purpose or vendor…** box to find a specific one.
- Use the **Category** dropdown to narrow it down further.
- Click any row (or request number) to open its full detail page, which shows every field, its receipts, its approval chain, and a full timeline of what's happened to it.

**Tip:** the search box matches the request number, the purpose text, or the vendor name — so if you remember roughly what it was for, you can find it even without the exact number.

## Approving Expenses

*This section applies to Managers, Finance, and Admins.* If you're an Employee, **Approvals** won't appear in your menu — you can track your own submissions from **Requests** instead.

### How approvals are routed

- **Managers** only see and can approve requests from people in **their own department**.
- **Finance and Admin** can see and approve **any** pending request in the organization.
- You can never approve your own request, no matter your role.
- Depending on your organization's settings, a request may need a manager's approval, a finance review, or both, before it's fully approved — and any request above a set "finance review" amount always gets a finance review, even if that isn't normally required. Requests below the auto-approve threshold skip this entirely.

### How to approve

**When to use this:** you've been notified (or you see it on your **Approvals** page) that someone is waiting on your decision, and you're satisfied it's a legitimate business expense.

**Steps:**
1. Click **Approvals** in the left menu — it lists everything awaiting your review, and shows the requester, amount, category, vendor, and priority for each.
2. Optionally click **View Details** to see the full request, its receipts, and history first.
3. Click **Approve**. You can add an optional comment first (from the request's own page — the Approvals list itself approves with no comment).

**What happens next:** the request becomes **Approved**, the employee is notified, and (for personal-payment expenses) it now waits for Finance or an Admin to process payment.

### How to reject

**Steps:**
1. From **Approvals**, click **Reject** on the request (or **Reject** from its detail page).
2. A dialog opens asking you to **explain why this request is being rejected**. This is required — Monytar won't let you reject without a reason.
3. Click **Reject request** (or **Confirm Rejection** on the detail page) to finish.

**What happens next:** the request becomes **Rejected**, and the employee sees your reason and can revise and resubmit it.

## Getting Paid Back (Payments)

Monytar doesn't automatically wire money to anyone. "Processing payment" in Monytar means **recording that your company already paid the person back outside of Monytar** — by bank transfer, check, cash, or however your finance team actually pays.

**Who can do this:** Finance and Admin only, and only once a request is **Approved**.

**Steps:**
1. Open the approved request.
2. Click **Process Payment**.
3. Enter a **Payment Reference** (for example, a bank transfer ID — something like `TXN-12345`). This is required.
4. Choose the **Payment Method**: Bank Transfer, Credit Card, Check, or Cash.
5. Click **Confirm Payment**.

**What happens next:** the request's status changes to **Paid**, and its Payment Status and Payment Method are recorded on the request permanently. The employee is notified.

> **Reimbursement** means getting money back for a business expense you paid for yourself. In Monytar, that's simply: your request gets Approved, then Finance/Admin marks it Paid once the money has actually gone out through your company's normal payment process.

## Vendors

A **Vendor** is anyone or any company your organization pays — a software provider, a supplier, a contractor, etc. Registering vendors means requests can reference "who was paid" consistently instead of everyone typing it differently.

Click **Vendors** in the left menu. Everyone can view and use vendors on their requests; **Admin, Finance, and Manager** can add and manage them.

### How to add a vendor

1. Click **Add vendor**.
2. Enter the **Name** (required — must be unique) and optionally a **Category**, **Email**, **Phone**, **Address**, **Payment terms**, and **Notes**.
3. Click **Add vendor**.

New vendors are automatically **Approved** and immediately selectable on expense requests.

### Approving, archiving, and searching vendors

- Each vendor card shows an **Approved**/**Pending** badge and a toggle to flip it. Only **unapproved** vendors won't show up as options on new requests.
- Click the archive icon on a card (visible on hover, or always visible on touch) to **Archive** a vendor you no longer use. Archived vendors stay on past requests but can't be picked for new ones — you can restore them any time with **Show archived**.
- Use the search box to find a vendor by name or category.

## Departments and Budgets

A **Department** groups people together (like "Engineering" or "Sales") and gives that group a spending budget Monytar tracks against.

Click **Departments** in the left menu. *Employees don't have access to this page.* Managers can view it; **Admin and Finance** can create, edit, and archive departments and assign people to them (assigning people is Admin-only).

### How department budgets work

Each department has a **Budget** amount and a **Budget period** (Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly). Every department card shows a progress bar of how much has been spent against that budget, and a percentage. As spending climbs:

- Under the organization's warning threshold: shown in green, no label.
- At the warning threshold: **Warning**, shown in amber.
- At the critical threshold or above: **Critical** or **Over budget**, shown in red.

(Your organization sets these thresholds — the typical defaults are 75% for warning, 90% for critical, and 100% for over budget.)

### How to create a department

1. Click **Add department**.
2. Enter a **Name** (required, must be unique) and optional **Description**.
3. Enter a **Budget** in your organization's currency (required — enter `0` if you just want to track spending with no real limit) and pick the **Budget period**.
4. Optionally assign a **Manager**.
5. Click **Create department**.

### Managing who's in a department

Click **Members** on any department card to see who's assigned. Admins can pick someone from the dropdown and click **Add** — this moves them out of any department they were in before, since everyone belongs to at most one department at a time. Click **Remove** next to a person to take them out.

### Archiving a department

Click **Archive** on a department card. Archived departments stay on past requests and reports, and anyone already assigned to one keeps that assignment, but the department won't be selectable for new requests or new assignments. Click **Show archived** to see them, and **Restore** to bring one back.

## Reports

*Reports are only available to Finance and Admin.*

Click **Reports** in the left menu. At the top, four summary cards show **Total Submitted**, **Total Approved**, **Total Paid**, and **Avg. Request** for whichever date range you've picked (**Last 30 days**, **Last 3 months**, **Last 6 months**, **Last 12 months**, or **All time** — the dropdown next to Export).

Four tabs dig deeper:

- **Overview** — a monthly trend chart (submitted vs. approved vs. paid) and a status breakdown pie chart.
- **Categories** — spending by category, as a bar chart and a detailed list with percentages.
- **Departments** — each department's budget usage as a bar chart, a "Budget Health" list with percentages, and a ranked table of departments by total spend.
- **Vendors** — a ranked table of your top vendors by total spend, with each one's share of total spending.

### Exporting a report

Click **Export**, then choose:

- **Download CSV** — a spreadsheet-ready file you can open in Excel or Google Sheets.
- **Print / Save as PDF** — opens a print-friendly report you can print or save as a PDF from your browser's print dialog.

Both exports reflect whatever date range is currently selected.

## Notifications

Click the bell icon in the top bar, or **Notifications** in the left menu, to see everything Monytar has told you about: requests approved or rejected, payments processed, budget alerts, new vendors, and new teammates invited.

- Unread notifications have a solid dot next to them and a slightly highlighted background.
- Click a notification to mark it read — if it's about a specific request, it also takes you straight to that request.
- Click **Mark read** next to an individual one, or **Mark all read** at the top of the page, to clear them without opening each one.
- Use the tabs at the top of the **Notifications** page — **All**, **Unread**, **Approved**, **Rejected**, **Payments**, **Alerts** — to filter.

## Searching Monytar

Beyond the search boxes on individual pages (Requests, Approvals, Vendors, Users), Monytar has a global search you can open from anywhere:

- Click the **Search…** box in the top bar (desktop), or the search icon (mobile).
- Or press **Ctrl+K** (Windows/Linux) or **⌘K** (Mac) on your keyboard.

Type to jump straight to a page, or to find a specific request, vendor, user, or department by name — whatever your role can actually see. Press **Enter** or click a result to go there.

## Your Profile and Settings

Click **Settings** in the left menu, or your name in the top-right account menu. Everyone has access, but which tabs do something depends on your role.

### Profile tab

- **Personal Information** — update your **Full name** and click **Save Changes**. Your **Email** is shown but can't be changed here — it's your sign-in identity.
- **Security** — change your password. Enter your **Current Password** and **New Password** (at least 8 characters) and click **Update Password**. If your account requires it, you'll then be asked to enter a **Confirmation Code** that was emailed to you, and click **Confirm New Password**.

### Organization tab

Everyone can *see* this tab; only **Admin** and **Finance** can change most of it.

- **Organization Details** — your organization's name, plan, auto-approve threshold, receipt threshold, and fiscal year start, for reference.
- **Logo** — Admins can **Upload** or **Replace** the company logo (JPG, PNG, WEBP, or SVG, up to 2 MB), and remove it.
- **Currency** — Admin/Finance pick the organization's **Default Currency** from the dropdown. A preview shows exactly how amounts will display. Everyone else sees this but can't change it.
- **Expense Categories** — shows the six built-in categories (Travel, Meals & Entertainment, Software & Services, Equipment, Office Supplies, Other) plus any your organization has added. On Starter plan or higher, Admin/Finance can type a name and click **Add** to create a new one, or remove a custom one they added (built-in ones can't be removed). On the Free plan, this is locked with a note to upgrade.
- **Approval Workflow** — Admin/Finance set:
  - **Auto-approve under** — requests below this amount skip approval entirely.
  - **Receipt required above** — a receipt must be attached above this amount.
  - **Finance review above** — requests at or above this amount always get a finance review.
  - Toggles for whether **Manager approval**, **Finance review**, and the **Receipt requirement** are required at all.
  - Click **Save Changes** to apply.

### Notifications tab

Turn on/off **Approval Notifications**, **Rejection Notifications**, **Payment Notifications**, and **Push Notifications**, and choose an **Email Digest Frequency** (Real-time, Daily Digest, Weekly Digest, or None).

### Appearance tab

Pick **Light**, **Dark**, or **System** (matches your device) to change how Monytar looks.

## Admin Guide

*Everything below is only available to the Admin role, except where noted.*

### Managing users

Click **Users** in the left menu.

**Inviting someone:**
1. Click **Invite user**.
2. Enter their **Email**, pick a **Role**, and optionally a **Department**.
3. Click **Send invitation**. If you're inviting a Manager, assign them a department so they can actually approve their team's requests.

Monytar emails them a link to set their own name and password (that's why there's no "name" field here — see [Getting Your Account Set Up](#getting-your-account-set-up)). Until they accept, they appear under **Pending invitations** at the bottom of the page, where you can **Copy link** (to share it yourself) or **Revoke** the invitation.

> If the invitation email couldn't be sent for some reason, Monytar tells you honestly and copies the invite link to your clipboard so you can share it directly instead.

**Editing someone:** click **Edit** next to a user to change their **Role** or **Department**. You can't change your own role (ask another admin), and you can't demote the organization's *only* active admin — promote someone else first.

**Deactivating someone:** click **Deactivate** to immediately revoke their access — they can no longer sign in, though their past requests stay in your records. Click **Reactivate** to restore access at any time. You can't deactivate yourself, and you can't deactivate the only active admin.

### Managing departments, vendors, and categories

Covered above in [Departments and Budgets](#departments-and-budgets), [Vendors](#vendors), and [Settings → Organization tab](#organization-tab) — Admin (and Finance, for most of these) manage them all from those same pages.

### Organization settings

Covered in [Settings → Organization tab](#organization-tab) — logo, currency, expense categories, and the approval workflow thresholds.

### Billing (Admin & Finance)

Under **Settings → Billing**:

- **Current Plan** shows your active plan, its price, and its features. Click **Manage Billing** to open Stripe's secure billing portal, where you can update payment methods, download invoices, or change your plan.
- **Change Plan** lists every available plan as a card. Click **Upgrade**, **Downgrade**, or **Select** on any plan to switch — changes take effect immediately (moving to a paid plan for the first time sends you through a short checkout). Enterprise has no self-serve button; click **Contact Sales** instead.
- **Cancel Subscription** cancels your paid plan. You keep paid features until the end of your current billing period, then automatically move to the Free plan.

### Demo Leads (Admin only)

**Demo Leads** is a separate item that appears in Admin's tools — it lists people who filled out Monytar's public "Try the demo" marketing form on the website, not anything related to your own organization's expenses. Most admins won't need this day to day.

## "What Should I Do?" Quick Answers

**I paid for something myself. What should I do?**
→ Go to **Requests → New request**, fill it in, attach your receipt, and click **Submit for Approval**. Once it's **Approved**, Finance or an Admin will mark it **Paid** once they've actually sent you the money.

**My expense was rejected. What should I do?**
→ Open the request, read the **Reviewer Comment**, click **Edit** to fix the issue, then click **Resubmit**.

**I forgot to attach my receipt. What should I do?**
→ If the request is still a **Draft** or has been **Rejected**, open it, click **Edit**, and add the receipt under **Add More Receipts**. If it's already **Pending** or further along, you can no longer edit it yourself — ask whoever is reviewing it, or Finance/Admin, for help.

**I need to find an old expense.**
→ Go to **Requests**, use the search box (matches request number, purpose, or vendor) and the status/category filters. Or press **Ctrl+K** / **⌘K** anywhere to jump straight to it.

**I need to approve an expense.**
→ Go to **Approvals** in the left menu (only visible if you're a Manager, Finance, or Admin) and click **Approve** or **Reject**.

**I need to pay someone back.**
→ Open their **Approved** request and click **Process Payment** (Finance/Admin only).

**I want to change what currency amounts show in.**
→ Go to **Settings → Organization** and change **Default Currency** (Admin/Finance only — it affects the whole organization, not just you).

**I need to add a new teammate.**
→ Go to **Users → Invite user** (Admin only).

**A request I should be able to approve isn't showing up.**
→ Check your role and department. Managers only see requests from people in their *own* department — if the requester is in a different department, only Finance or an Admin can approve it.

## Troubleshooting

### "I can't submit my expense."

**What to check:**
1. Both **Amount** and **Business Justification** must be filled in — Monytar blocks submission without them.
2. If your organization requires a receipt above a certain amount and your expense is over it, you'll see a warning and need to attach one before you can submit — or click **Save as Draft** instead and add it later.
3. Make sure the Amount is a positive number.

**If it still doesn't work:** check the toast message that appears at the bottom of the screen — Monytar explains what went wrong in plain language. If it says something about the connection, check your internet and try again.

### "I don't see a button/page I need."

This is almost always your role. Check who you're signed in as (bottom of the left menu). Pages like **Approvals**, **Departments**, **Users**, and **Reports** are only shown to certain roles (see [Roles in Monytar](#roles-in-monytar)). If you land on a restricted page directly, Monytar shows an explanation of which role is needed and offers a button back to your Dashboard.

### "My teammate's invitation isn't working."

Check **Users → Pending invitations**. If it says **Expired**, revoke it and send a new one. If the email never arrived, use **Copy link** and share the invitation link with them directly (by chat, email, whatever's convenient).

### "I can't change my password."

Make sure your new password is at least 8 characters, and that you've typed your *current* password correctly first — Monytar re-checks it before allowing a change. If Monytar asks for a confirmation code, check your email (including spam) for it; the code is required to finish.

### "The amounts look wrong / in the wrong currency."

Amounts always display in your organization's configured currency. If that's wrong, ask an Admin or Finance user to update it under **Settings → Organization → Currency**.

### "A department's budget shows as red/over budget."

That's not an error — it means spending has crossed your organization's warning, critical, or over-budget threshold for that department. Talk to that department's manager or Finance about it; Monytar doesn't block new requests just because a budget is over, it only flags it.

## Glossary

- **Request (expense request)** — the record of one expense you've submitted, from creation through payment.
- **Reimbursement** — getting money back for a business expense you paid for yourself.
- **Draft** — a request you've started but not yet submitted.
- **Auto-approve threshold** — the amount under which a request skips approval entirely.
- **Approval chain** — the sequence of people/roles (manager, then finance, or both) who need to sign off on a request before it's approved.
- **Vendor** — a company or person your organization pays.
- **Department** — a team with its own spending budget.
- **Budget period** — how often a department's budget resets: Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly.
- **Payment reference** — the identifier (like a bank transfer ID) recorded when Finance/Admin marks a request Paid.
- **Category** — the type of expense (Travel, Meals & Entertainment, Software & Services, Equipment, Office Supplies, Other, or a custom one your organization added).

## Internal Documentation Notes

*Developer-facing observations from reviewing the codebase. Not for end users.*

- **"Cancelled" status exists but has no UI trigger.** `RequestStatus` includes `"cancelled"` (`lib/types.ts`), it's handled in report aggregation (`lib/reports/aggregate.ts`) and recognized as a URL filter value on the Requests page, but no button or action anywhere in the UI sets a request to `cancelled` — nor is it one of the status filter tabs on the Requests page (only All/Drafts/Pending/Approved/Rejected/Paid). Needs verification whether this is dead/future functionality or an intentional gap.
- **No card, bank account, or transaction-feed features exist.** There is no `Card`, `BankAccount`, or `Transaction` type or table anywhere in the codebase. "Payment Method" on a request is just a manual dropdown (Bank Transfer / Credit Card / Check / Cash) recorded when Finance/Admin marks it Paid — it does not connect to any real bank or card feed. The user guide above intentionally does not claim any card/transaction features exist.
- **No integrations feature exists.** "Custom integrations" is marketing copy for the Enterprise plan (`lib/products.ts`, `app/pricing/page.tsx`) but there is no integrations page, settings, or connector anywhere in the app.
- **No AI features exist.** No AI/LLM-related functionality was found anywhere in the app-facing code.
- **No recurring-expense feature exists.** Every request is a one-off submission; there's no template or recurrence scheduling.
- **Priority field has no behavioral effect found.** `priority` ("low"/"medium"/"high"/"urgent") is captured on the request and shown to reviewers, but nothing in `lib/approvals/engine.ts` or elsewhere changes routing, SLAs, or notifications based on it. It appears to be informational only. Worth confirming with product whether it was meant to do more.
- **Settings → Billing tab role visibility.** The Billing tab content itself renders for every role (the "Manage Billing" / "Change Plan" / "Cancel Subscription" *action buttons* are gated to `isAdminOrFinance`, but the tab and read-only plan info are not gated). The user guide notes this as "needs verification" rather than asserting an exact role list for tab *visibility* vs. tab *content*, since I could not find an explicit access check hiding the tab itself from Employee/Manager.
- **Password minimum length inconsistency.** `lib/validations.ts`'s `signInSchema`/base password zod schema uses `.min(6, ...)`, but every actual UI surface (signup, accept-invitation, Settings password change) enforces 8 characters client-side. Not user-visible today, but worth aligning the schema to 8 if it's ever used server-side for validation without the client-side check in front of it.
- **Demo Leads is unrelated to org expense data.** It's a CRM-like list of marketing-site `/demo` form submissions (`demo_leads` table), scoped to Admin only, and was deliberately given a light mention rather than folded into the main task-based guide, since it isn't part of the expense-management workflow real users care about.
- **"Teams" vs "Departments."** The spec's outline mentions "Teams/departments" as if they might be separate concepts; Monytar only has **Departments** — there is no separate "Teams" entity.
- **Screenshots.** No image assets, Storybook, or design files documenting UI were found in the repo to reference; all UI wording in this guide was taken directly from the current component source (`app/(dashboard)/**/page.tsx`, `components/**`) as of this review.

## Keeping the in-app Help page current

The signed-in **Help** page (`/help`) is the interactive, screenshot-based version of this guide. Its screenshots
are real screens of the app with numbered markers, generated by a script rather than drawn by hand:

```bash
pnpm dev                                         # in one terminal
node scripts/capture-help-screenshots.mjs        # in another
```

The script seeds a throwaway demo organization, signs in as each role in Google Chrome, saves the images to
`public/images/help/`, writes marker positions to `components/help/markers.json`, and deletes everything it
created. Re-run it (and commit the results) whenever a screen shown in the Help page changes. The wording of the
steps lives in `components/help/guide-data.ts`.

## The shareable Monytar Handbook

`/handbook` is a public, interactive handbook that can be shared by link (it is `noindex`, so search engines skip it),
and `public/downloads/Monytar-Handbook.pdf` is its printable edition. Because it leaves the company, it may only say
what a person using the product can already see on their own screen: **no technology, vendors, infrastructure,
security design, internal numbers, customer names, or real people/emails.**

- Copy lives in `components/handbook/handbook-content.ts` (plus the shared `components/help/guide-data.ts`).
- `tests/handbook-safe.test.ts` scans all of that copy for banned terms and contact details and fails the build if one slips in.
- The "Getting started" screenshots (sign-up and invitation) come from `node scripts/capture-handbook-start.mjs`. It opens the real pages with pretend server replies, so no account or email is ever created; markers go to `components/handbook/start-markers.json`.
- After changing the handbook copy or screenshots, regenerate the PDF and commit it:

```bash
pnpm dev                                  # in one terminal
node scripts/export-handbook-pdf.mjs      # in another
```
