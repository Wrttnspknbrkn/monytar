Living checklist. Severity: **P0** security/data-loss/critical blocker · **P1** major broken functionality · **P2** significant UX/functionality gap · **P3** polish.

Status: `Open` · `In Progress` · `Fixed (unverified)` · `Verified` · `Won't Fix (documented)` · `Needs External Config`

All three research agents (RBAC/tenant-isolation, financial-workflow/reporting, CRUD/UX) have completed and their findings are merged below. Migrations `018`–`020` are applied. Every P0 and the flagship currency bug have been live-verified against the real database and the running app (see each row's Notes) — test data created for verification was deleted afterward and confirmed gone.

**Update**: migrations `021` (billing interval + realigned tier seat limits) and `022` (expense categories) and `023` (org logo storage) are also applied.
